# Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 20, 2025

## 1. Overview

BA Torment (hereafter "the Service") is an unofficial fan site for Blue Archive. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Service collects and how it is handled.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Automatically Collected

- Visit history and usage patterns (Google Analytics)
- Device information, including browser type and operating system
- IP address (anonymized)

### 2.2 Provided by the User

- Gemini API key entered for A.R.O.N.A (stored temporarily in session storage and deleted ten minutes after the last chat activity)

## 3. How We Use the Information

- Provide and improve the Service
- Analyze usage statistics
- Maintain Service stability

## 4. Third-Party Services

The Service uses the following third-party services:

- **Google Analytics**: visit statistics
- **YouTube API**: video analysis features

The Service does not display any ads. For details on each service's data handling, please refer to its respective privacy policy.

## 5. Cookies

The Service uses cookies to improve user experience and analyze usage. You can refuse cookies in your browser settings, but some features may be limited.

## 6. Data Retention & Deletion

- Gemini API key: stored temporarily in session storage and deleted ten minutes after the last chat activity
- Analytics data: follows the Google Analytics retention policy

## 7. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact [haulrest@gmail.com](mailto:haulrest@gmail.com).
