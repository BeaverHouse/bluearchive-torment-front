# BA Torment Site Guide

BA Torment lets you explore Total Assault and Grand Assault data through **parties, students, records, and videos**.

![BA Torment home page](/data/pages/images/guide-home.png)

## Party Finder & Summary

Choose a season at the top, then use the three tabs:

- **Party Finder** finds real clear parties that match your conditions.
- **Summary** shows the Platinum cutoff, key students, common parties, and special clears at a glance.
- **Trends** shows how scores and usage patterns changed across seasons.

![Party Finder page](/data/pages/images/guide-party.png)

### Which search mode should I use?

- **Filters** are best when you want precise conditions such as included or excluded students, party count, score, assistants, or video availability.
- **Student Pool** lets you register the students you own and find parties you can actually build. You can also allow missing students and choose how strictly star levels must match.
- **Single Party** is best for quickly comparing one composition with its usage records and alternatives.

### Filter examples

Filters are most useful when you cannot build a top-ranked party exactly as shown.

- **Missing one or two key students:** Register the students you own in Student Pool, then allow one or two missing students. The missing slots in each result help you decide whom to borrow and whom to build next.
- **Your main dealer is underbuilt:** Select the dealer you plan to borrow as the assistant, then add your own supports under included students. For example, if your Dress Hina is underbuilt for S90 Gregorius Torment, you can keep only records that borrowed Dress Hina.
- **You want consistency instead of the highest score:** In Grand Assault S29 Kaitenger, a one-party Maid Aris clear was the fastest Special Armor option, while Swimsuit Mika was more consistent. Excluding Maid Aris or including Swimsuit Mika reveals practical alternatives hidden behind the top-scoring party.
- **A one-party clear is out of reach:** Expand the party-count range to two or more. In the same Kaitenger season, many clears used an area dealer for phase one and saved a single-target dealer for the robot.

If no results remain, relax the missing-student allowance, star policy, and party count one at a time. This makes it easier to see which condition removed the viable records.

Filters and student pools can be copied or downloaded as **presets**. Import them when moving to another browser or device.

## Students

Search the student list by name or sort it by usage rank or name. A student page brings together:

- Total Assault and Grand Assault usage and investment statistics
- Students commonly used together
- Skill investment recommendations based on observed clears
- Season reports and build notes that mention the student

Skill recommendations summarize observed investment patterns. They are not absolute answers for every piece of content.

## Arona's Archive

Arona's Archive has two views: **Ask Arona** and **Browse Records**.

![Arona's Archive](/data/pages/images/guide-archive.png)

- Browse Records lets you search and read beginner guides, build notes, boss guides, and season reports.
- Ask Arona accepts natural-language questions about students, skills, parties, and raid records.
- Asking questions requires your own Gemini API key. The key is encrypted in browser session storage and deleted ten minutes after your last chat activity.

If an answer looks wrong, open the related record to verify it or use the feedback form at the bottom of the document.

## Video Analysis

Total Assault and Grand Assault clear videos are collected and analyzed automatically. You can browse them by season and inspect the detected scores and parties. The operator currently handles corrections and verification manually.

If a video's score, students, star levels, or party composition is wrong, submit a corrected analysis through **Edit** on its detail page. Even one correction helps us resolve automatic-analysis errors much faster. The editor requires a wider screen and is available on PC.

## Calculators

The score calculator estimates a Total Assault or Grand Assault score from the difficulty and remaining time. You can switch to the Tactical Challenge time calculator on the same page.

## Language & Appearance

Use the language button in the header to switch between Korean, English, and Chinese, and the theme button to switch between light and dark mode. Some guide documents may remain in Korean when no translation is available.
