# Release Notes

Only what teachers need to know, recorded from October 2026.

## 2026-10-12

### Arona

- You can now ask Arona without an API key.
  - Today's free answers are shown as Pyroxene. Each question uses what it costs, and Pyroxene refills every day.
  - When Pyroxene runs out, set a Gemini API key to keep asking.
- Ask Arona straight from the home page. Press Shift+Enter for a new line.
- For questions that need interpretation, such as guides, terms and recommendations, Arona reads the records before answering.
- Arona no longer makes up the meaning of a nickname or phrase she cannot verify. She says she could not confirm it.

### Arona's Archive

- The record list is grouped by kind (basics, boss guides, season reports, builds), and each group folds. A search opens every group that has a match.

## 2026-10-03

### Party Finder and Videos

- Total Assault and Grand Assault lists show the newest season first. Party Finder opens on the newest season with party data, and a returning visitor sees the season they viewed last.
- Ongoing and upcoming seasons are labelled. A season without videos yet tells you why.
- A video analysis shows whether it is the AI result or a checked analysis.
- You can report a mistake in a video analysis.
- Video URLs in the analysis queue are links.

## 2026-10-01

- Fixed the English and Chinese names of the DRUMBARKA and Gregorius raids.
